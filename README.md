## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[Nextflow](https://github.com/nextflow-io/nextflow)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nextflow-io/nextflow?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/nextflow-io/nextflow/stargazers)  

  **Data-driven computational pipelines for bioinformatics**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **3K+ GitHub stars** — **the de facto standard for bioinformatics pipelines** . **Used by 100K+ researchers worldwide** . **Portable across cloud and on-premises** . **Container-native (Docker, Singularity)** . **Reproducible, scalable, and auditable** . **Seqera Platform provides enterprise management** . **The definitive open-source bioinformatics pipeline framework** . 🧬



- **[Bioconductor](https://github.com/Bioconductor/BiocManager)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Bioconductor/BiocManager?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Bioconductor/BiocManager/stargazers)  

  **Open-source R ecosystem for genomics and bioinformatics**, Artistic-2.0 licensed. **The foundational R package ecosystem** — **2,000+ packages** for **RNA-Seq, single-cell, ChIP-Seq, and multi-omics analysis** . **The standard for statistical genomics** . 📊



- **[Galaxy Project](https://github.com/galaxyproject/galaxy)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/galaxyproject/galaxy?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/galaxyproject/galaxy/stargazers)  

  **Open-source bioinformatics workflow platform**, Academic Free License. **1.4K+ GitHub stars** — **the most widely used open-source bioinformatics platform** . **Thousands of tools for genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics** . **Web-based interface for wet-lab scientists** . **The most accessible open-source bioinformatics platform** . 🌌



- **[Snakemake](https://github.com/snakemake/snakemake)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/snakemake/snakemake?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/snakemake/snakemake/stargazers)  

  **Workflow management system for reproducible data analysis**, MIT licensed. **2K+ GitHub stars** — **Python-based workflow definition** . **The most Pythonic bioinformatics workflow engine** . 🐍



- **[GATK (Genome Analysis Toolkit)](https://github.com/broadinstitute/gatk)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/broadinstitute/gatk?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/broadinstitute/gatk/stargazers)  

  **The gold standard for variant discovery**, BSD-3-Clause licensed. **The most widely used variant calling toolkit** . **Developed by Broad Institute** . **Best practices for germline and somatic variant calling** . 🧬



- **[Samtools](https://github.com/samtools/samtools)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/samtools/samtools?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/samtools/samtools/stargazers)  

  **Utilities for manipulating SAM/BAM/CRAM files**, MIT licensed. **The foundational toolkit for genomics data manipulation** . **Used by virtually every genomics pipeline** . 🛠️



- **[BCFtools](https://github.com/samtools/bcftools)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/samtools/bcftools?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/samtools/bcftools/stargazers)  

  **Utilities for variant calling and manipulating VCF/BCF files**, MIT licensed. **The standard for VCF manipulation** . **Used with GATK and other variant callers** . 📋



- **[HISAT2](https://github.com/DaehwanKimLab/hisat2)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/DaehwanKimLab/hisat2?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/DaehwanKimLab/hisat2/stargazers)  

  **Graph-based alignment of next-generation sequencing reads**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most widely used RNA-Seq aligner** . **Ultrafast and sensitive alignment** . 🧪



- **[STAR](https://github.com/alexdobin/STAR)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/alexdobin/STAR?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/alexdobin/STAR/stargazers)  

  **Spliced Transcripts Alignment to a Reference**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most accurate RNA-Seq aligner** . **Used by ENCODE and GTEx** . ⭐



- **[DESeq2](https://github.com/mikelove/DESeq2)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mikelove/DESeq2?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/mikelove/DESeq2/stargazers)  

  **Differential gene expression analysis**, LGPL-3.0 licensed. **The standard for RNA-Seq differential expression** . **Used by thousands of researchers** . 📈



- **[Seurat](https://github.com/satijalab/seurat)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/satijalab/seurat?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/satijalab/seurat/stargazers)  

  **Toolkit for single-cell genomics**, MIT licensed. **The most widely used single-cell analysis platform** . **Used by 100K+ researchers** . 🔬



- **[Scanpy](https://github.com/scverse/scanpy)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/scverse/scanpy?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/scverse/scanpy/stargazers)  

  **Single-cell analysis in Python**, BSD-3-Clause licensed. **The Python-native alternative to Seurat** . **Scalable to millions of cells** . 🐍



- **[MultiQC](https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/MultiQC/MultiQC?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC/stargazers)  

  **Aggregate bioinformatics results across many samples**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The standard for bioinformatics QC reporting** . 📊



- **[nf-core](https://github.com/nf-core/tools)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nf-core/tools?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/nf-core/tools/stargazers)  

  **Curated Nextflow pipelines for bioinformatics**, MIT licensed. **100+ production-ready pipelines** . **The community standard for reproducible bioinformatics** . 🚀

## 🛠️ How to Contribute



Contributions are welcome! Follow these steps to submit new omics analytics platforms or open-source bioinformatics software:



1. 🍴 **Fork** the repository.

2. 📝 **Add/edit** entries in `README.md` maintaining table/list structure and formatting.

3. 🔗 Include project title, official website/GitHub link, exact Stars_Count, license, and brief description.

4. 🚀 Submit a **Pull Request** with a descriptive summary of your changes.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Nextflow is the de facto standard for bioinformatics pipelines** with **3K+ GitHub stars** and **used by 100K+ researchers worldwide** . **Galaxy is the most widely used open-source bioinformatics platform** with **1.4K+ GitHub stars** . **Bioconductor is the foundational R ecosystem** with **2,000+ packages** .
